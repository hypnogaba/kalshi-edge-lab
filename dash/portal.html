What we set out to solve

The Latency Race

Same Bitcoin market, two pipes side by side — the free public Kalshi feed vs. the DoubleZero edge feed — with a millisecond counter on who sees each trade first. Output: a clip and a table nobody can argue with.

The Demo Bot

A deliberately simple bot on Kalshi's demo environment. It reads real BTC trades and Binance spot, forms a signal, and would place fake-money orders — the whole pipeline, live on a dashboard.

Live · running on real data
